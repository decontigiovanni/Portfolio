Shipping & Preorder Policy

Home / Lab / Shipping & Delivery

Pricing & Estimated Delivery Time

  • Italy: €17 via standard courier (1–2 weeks)
  • Europe: €17 via DHL (1–2 weeks)
  • Netherlands: €8 via standard courier (1–2 weeks)
  • UK: €30 via DHL (1–2 weeks)
  • Rest of the World: Price displayed at checkout (1–2 weeks)

Delivery times may vary slightly depending on my availability, as each item is handcrafted. This is a one-man project, and every order is prepared individually.

Preorders

  • All products in the Lab section are available for preorder.
  • Orders will be shipped within 1–2 weeks from confirmation.
  • You will receive a confirmation email with the tracking number once your package is ready for shipment.

Duties and Taxes

  • Prices do not include import taxes or customs fees.
  • I have no control over additional charges outside the EU; costs depend on your local customs policies.
  • It is recommended to check with your local customs office before placing an order.

Orders

  • Orders confirmed before 12:00 PM (GMT+1) will be prepared the same day, unless production constraints occur.
  • Orders confirmed after 12:00 PM (GMT+1) will be shipped the next day or within the indicated time frame.

Returns / Refunds

  • Returns or refunds are not accepted.
  • This is a small, handcrafted side project; by purchasing, you accept the product as-is.
  • Each bag is unique and handmade; buyers acknowledge the one-of-a-kind nature of the item.
